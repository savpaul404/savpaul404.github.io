Consumer FAQs

What is Trewda? Is it a lender?

Trewda is a financial technology platform that connects consumers, merchants, and participating financial institutions. Trewda is not a lender. Financial institutions provide financing products and make eligibility, approval, and terms decisions.

About Trewda
What types of purchases can I finance through Trewda?

Categories include home improvement and home repair, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, automotive repair, veterinary services, animal training, and much more. Availability depends on participating merchants and financial partners.

Explore purchase categories
How do I get started?

Start with a participating merchant or financing experience. Review any eligible options presented by the financial institution and its terms before deciding whether to proceed.

See how Trewda works
How much can I finance?

Financing amounts depend on the purchase, the financial institution's products, and its eligibility review. Trewda does not promise a minimum or maximum financing amount.

Review financing information
Does exploring financing affect my credit?

That depends on the financial institution and the type of review it performs. Review the provider's credit-check disclosures before proceeding; Trewda does not promise that a credit check will have no impact.

Understand financing options
Is approval guaranteed?

No. Each participating financial institution reviews applications using its own criteria. Not every application receives an offer, and available options vary.

Understand the financing process
What rates, repayment terms, or fees are available?

Rates, terms, and fees vary by provider, product, purchase, and applicant. Review the financial institution's disclosures and agreement before accepting an option. Trewda does not guarantee rates or fee-free financing.

Learn about provider terms
Can I pay off financing early, and who receives the funds?

Early repayment rules, possible fees, payment arrangements, and funding timing depend on your financial institution and agreement. Ask the provider and merchant to confirm these details before you accept financing.

Review your financing options
Who should I contact about an existing financing account?

Contact the financial institution that provided your financing for balances, payments, servicing, or account-specific questions. For questions about the Trewda platform, contact Trewda support.

Contact Trewda
Where can I learn about privacy and electronic consent?

Trewda's legal pages explain information practices and electronic consent. Review the Privacy Policy and related disclosures, along with the financial institution's own notices when considering financing.

Read the Privacy Policy

Merchant FAQs

What does Trewda offer merchants?

Trewda provides technology that connects merchants and customers with participating financing providers. It supports financing within the sales experience while financial institutions supply the financing products.

Explore business solutions
What types of businesses can partner with Trewda?

Businesses in categories such as home improvement, repair services, automotive repair, veterinary services, and animal training can inquire about a partnership. Participation and available options depend on the business and participating financial partners.

Learn about merchant partnerships
How does financing fit into our sales process?

A participating merchant introduces financing as part of a purchase. Customers review any eligible options from participating providers and complete the provider's process. Integration details should be discussed with Trewda for your business.

See the merchant journey
Who makes lending decisions and how are merchants paid?

Participating financial institutions make lending decisions and provide financing, not Trewda or the merchant. Payment arrangements, responsibilities, and timing depend on the applicable provider and partnership agreements.

Review the merchant workflow
How do I become a partner, and how long does onboarding take?

Use the partnership inquiry on the Business Solutions page to prepare an email to Trewda. The team can discuss fit, requirements, and next steps. Onboarding timing varies; no fixed approval or launch timeline is promised.

Inquire about a partnership

Financial Partner FAQs

What role do financial partners play?

Participating financial institutions provide financing products and determine eligibility, approval, and applicable terms. Trewda supplies the technology connections between financial partners, merchants, and consumers.

Explore the connected platform
How can a financial institution discuss partnering with Trewda?

Contact Trewda and select Financial Institution Partnerships. The contact form prepares an email draft so you can share your inquiry with the team; it does not submit or store your information on the website.

Contact the partnership team
