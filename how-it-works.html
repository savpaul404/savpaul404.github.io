How It Works

One connected ecosystem.

Trewda facilitates financing interactions between consumers, merchants, and financial institutions. Participating financial institutions provide the underlying financing products.

The ecosystem

Trewda sits in the middle.

Consumers and merchants connect to Trewda. Trewda connects them to participating financial institutions. Each participant does what it does best.

  • Consumers ↔ Trewda ↔ Financial Institutions
  • Merchants ↔ Trewda ↔ Financial Institutions
Technology Platform

Select a participant to see how Trewda connects them.

The consumer journey

  1. 1 Shop with a participating merchant
  2. 2 Begin the Trewda financing experience
  3. 3 Review any eligible options from participating partners
  4. 4 Complete the provider's financing process

The merchant journey

  1. 1 Partner with Trewda
  2. 2 Bring financing into your sales process
  3. 3 Customers explore options with participating providers
  4. 4 Track financing activity with digital tools

Trewda is not the lender. Not every application will receive an offer, and the number of options presented varies.
